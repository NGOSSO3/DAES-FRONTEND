Our story

Why we built DAES

Digital Agriculture Enhancement System (DAES) began as a Young Scientist Tanzania (YST) research project by Enos John Ngosso and Thomas Edward Charle, developed and researched on the ground in Ilemela District, Mwanza Region. The project went on to qualify for the national YST exhibition in Dar es Salaam, in the agricultural science category.

We spent time talking to farmers around Ilemela about the crops, livestock and fish they actually raise — maize, beans, rice, tomatoes and spinach in the fields; cattle, chickens, pigs, goats, sheep and ducks in the homestead; and Nile perch and tilapia from the lake. Three problems came up again and again: farmers often lack structured, local growing guidance; weather surprises them at planting and harvest time; and market prices are hard to track, so middlemen often set the terms.

DAES answers all three in one place, in both English and Swahili: step-by-step education from land preparation to market day, daily and 5-day weather forecasts, live market price updates, and an AI assistant farmers can ask for quick advice — plus a direct line to an agricultural extension officer for anything the app can't answer.
